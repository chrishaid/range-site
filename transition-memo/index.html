What this covers

The memo, delivered

A board-ready briefing on your district’s own public data, built for an incoming leader’s first weeks.

  • A landscape, board-ready PDF briefing, one claim per page, built entirely from your state’s published education data.
  • A reproducible analysis script that regenerates every number in the memo directly from the raw state files, with no proprietary dependency.
  • Delivery no later than two weeks after a fully executed purchase order is received.
  • Editorial independence: EdTribune holds no district contract to renew and no stake in the answer.
